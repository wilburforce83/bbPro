Barrier Offset Settings

Barrier offset allows you to increase or decrease the return on a trade by moving the target price above or below the entry point. If you place a call trade lowering the barrier (default) makes it more likely to be ITM, but lowers the return. selecting inverse increases the barriers making it harder but increases the return.

NOTE: barrier limits vary, do not use barriers with auto-market selection. Be warned!

Cumulative Loss Martingale Settings

Cumulative Loss Martingale is something I came up with (possibly already out there somewhere too), to try and combat the exponential loss growth with classic martingale. It is still exponential, but less agressive. Instead of simply multiplying the last stake, it takes the total cumulative losses since the last winning trade + the initial stake, divided by the multiplier below. Default is 0.9.

Labouchere Settings

Labouchere is a fairly complex cancellation system whereby you make a list of numbers, your stake is equal to the sum of the first and last number in the list. If your trade windowsthe last number is taken off the list, if the trade loses, the value of the loss is added to the list of numbers. This continues until the list has been cleared, and it starts over again.

NOTE: Your stake is used to scale this list i.e. 0.50 stake will mean all numbers in the list are halved.

Seperate your list with commas ","
Ladder Profit Settings

Not to be confused with gambling's Stepladder betting system, the Ladder profit system is designed to remove the exponential losses faced with all Martingale type systems. The idea is that your stake will increase by a fixed multipler regardless of win or loss, until a fixed profit has been hit. Once the profit level is hit, the ladder is completed and the stake is reset to the intital stake and the process starts over again. To prevent excessive losses in a terrible market where you win rate drops very low, there is an additional multiplier used to calculate the maximum allowed stake. If the maximum stake ammount is hit the ladder fails and starts back at the initial stake again. It has proven to be quite robust in high frequency auto-trading where the win rate stays above 52% regularly.
