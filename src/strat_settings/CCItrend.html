Fine tune and adjust your trading triggers

In order to make a useful trigger for CCI we use a threshold e.g. 100, this is both the plus and minus trigger. In order to determine the direction we use a mean average of the last CCI figures as confirmation of direction. The longer average period the more accurate the direction , but also the slower the trigger reacts, this is known as indicator lag.
