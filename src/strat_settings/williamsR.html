Fine tune and adjust your trading triggers

Williams %R, also known as the Williams Percent Range, is a type of momentum indicator that moves between 0 and -100 and measures overbought and oversold levels. Triggers are created while moving past the two threshold figures. the Average is used to confirm momentum during that shift.

NOTE: threshold figures are negative, please use absolute numbers i.e. for -20 please enter 20.
